# NINEWORKS DESIGN LAB

Design research, papers, journal, and archive for Nineworks.

## Information Architecture

- Research — ongoing research projects and research logs
- Papers — working papers, theses, publications
- Journal — magazine, essays, notes, reviews
- Archive — visual experiments, systems, project studies
- Lab — profile, research areas, methodology

## Content IDs

- `R.001` Research
- `WP.001` Working Paper
- `P.001` Publication
- `J.001` Journal
- `A.001` Archive
- `REF.001` Reference
